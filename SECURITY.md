# Security Policy

StreamGive handles donation data and includes admin endpoints authenticated by
cryptographic signatures. We appreciate responsible reports that help us keep
donors, NGOs, and the service safe.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report suspected security vulnerabilities privately by emailing
**security@streamgive.org**. Do not open a public GitHub issue or disclose the
problem publicly before we have had a reasonable opportunity to investigate and
release a fix.

Include, where possible:

- a clear description of the vulnerability and its potential impact;
- the affected endpoint, component, commit, or configuration;
- reliable reproduction steps or a proof of concept; and
- any relevant logs, screenshots, or suggested mitigation.

Please avoid accessing, modifying, or deleting data that does not belong to
you. Stop testing if you encounter another person's private data and tell us
what happened in your report.

## What to expect

We will acknowledge receipt within five business days and keep the report
confidential while it is being investigated. We will work with the reporter to
understand the issue, determine its severity, and coordinate a fix and any
public disclosure. We may request additional information or ask for a
reasonable amount of time to remediate before disclosure.

Please do not include secrets, private keys, or unnecessary personal data in a
report. Reports made in good faith and in accordance with this policy will not
result in legal action by StreamGive for the testing activity itself.

## Scope

This policy covers this repository and the StreamGive backend services it
builds. Denial-of-service testing, social engineering, spam, and vulnerabilities
in third-party services are out of scope; please report those to the relevant
provider.
